Following an in-depth critical review received from the user, every checkable point

1. REAL FACTUAL ERROR FOUND: dbSNP138 date
------------------------------------------------------
The manuscript stated "dbSNP138 (2014)" everywhere (table titles, figures, text).
Verified via research: **dbSNP build 138 was officially released on October 23,
2013**, not 2014 (confirmed by the UCSC Genome Browser announcement of the same date).
The exact creation date of GATK's specific GRCh38 bundle (a liftover of that build)
could not be precisely verified (the VCF file contains no fileDate
field; the GCS bucket's timestamp, April 2021, is only a re-copy date).
=> To be corrected everywhere: "dbSNP138" without a wrongly-asserted year, or "dbSNP138
   (official build 2013-10-23; exact GRCh38 bundle assembly date not
   verified)".

2. "ALWAYS" CLAIM (merged_id > current_id) — 22 real counterexamples found
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verified across all 81,963 merge events (full coverage):
**22 real counterexamples** (0.027%) where the merged ID is NOT numerically
higher than the current ID. Examples: (current=73623650, merged_from=58444546),
(current=78688553, merged_from=4284902), etc.
=> The manuscript must replace "always" with "in 99.97% of cases (22 documented
   exceptions out of 81,963 merge events)" -- slightly weakens but does not invalidate
   the structural argument in §5.4 (1000G negative), which remains valid at this
   confidence level.

3. NUMERICAL RECONCILIATIONS -- all verified consistent (not bugs, a lack
   of clarity in the text)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 -- The "33 missing cases" (dbSNP144, §5.6): NOT a bug. Precisely verified:
  Original (naive, simple overwrite): current=2758, stale=105, absent=31
  Fixed (multi-ID per key)          : current=2725, stale=75, both=63, absent=31
  Of the 63 "both/ambiguous" cases: 33 came from the old "current", 30 from the old
  "stale" (33+30=63 exactly; 2758-33=2725; 105-30=75). 100% consistent, but
  this breakdown was never shown in the text -- to be added explicitly.

3.2 -- Unresolved indels (§5.7): NOT a bug, a missing category in the text.
  Verified: indel 138 -- "neither"=137, "absent"=79, total unresolved=216=1439-1223 (exact).
  SNV 138 -- "neither"=0, "absent"=7, total unresolved=7=2657-2650 (exact).
  The text only mentioned "neither", not "absent" separately -- to be fixed by
  explicitly presenting all 3 categories (resolved / neither / absent), never
  just 2 of 3.

3.3 -- "Indels less often stale" claim contradicted for dbSNP144: a REAL wording
  flaw. Exact figures (already measured): 138 -- SNV 8.26% vs indel 1.14%
  (indel less stale, confirmed); 144 -- SNV 0.69% vs indel 1.00% (indel MORE stale,
  a real reversal). Stating it as a global range masked this reversal.
  => To be rephrased per-bundle, never as a global range.

3.4 -- Asymmetry of 19 (absent, 138) vs 31 (absent, 144): INVESTIGATED, confirmed real
  and not a matching bug. Example verified directly on the raw file:
  chr20:17,772,881 T>G (rs241136) is present in dbSNP138 but ABSENT from dbSNP144
  (no line at all at this position in the file, not a representation issue).
  30 similar cases found. Interpretation: dbSNP138 (GATK bundle) and dbSNP144
  (GDC/NCI bundle) are two DIFFERENT export pipelines, not the same production
  chain at two dates -- part of the "138 vs 144" comparison therefore conflates
  temporal aging with a difference in curation/filtering criteria
  between GATK and GDC. To be explicitly stated as a limitation/potential confound.

5. PREDICTIVE DRIFT MODEL (§5.4/5.5 of the feedback) -- built and tested,
   HONEST NEGATIVE RESULT
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Naive model proposed by the reviewer: predicted stale rate(T) = fraction of
candidates (full unbiased population, 48,746) whose MOST RECENT merge date
postdates the bundle's freeze date T.

Result (freeze dates: 138=2013-10-23 official, 144=2015-06-03 verified in the
VCF header):
                          PREDICTED (naive model)   OBSERVED (full panel, 48,746)
  dbSNP138                    5.94%                    0.76%
  dbSNP144                    0.77%                    0.15%

=> The naive model OVER-PREDICTS the observed rate by a factor of ~7-8x for both
   bundles. Honest interpretation: either (a) the GATK dbSNP138 bundle's REAL
   assembly date is materially later than the build's official release date
   (2013-10-23) -- consistent with the uncertainty noted in point 1 above --,
   or (b) a binary "before/after" model is too coarse to capture the
   real drift process (a bundle export is not a perfect snapshot
   at a single date). Honest result to publish as-is (negative but informative),
   not hidden -- consistent with the style already established in Paper A/B.
