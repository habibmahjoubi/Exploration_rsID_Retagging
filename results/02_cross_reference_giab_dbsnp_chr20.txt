Real cross-reference: GIAB HG002 chr20 variants x dbSNP merge history — 2026-08-23
=======================================================================================

Sources:
  - Truth VCF: NIST/GIAB HG002_GRCh38_1_22_v4.2.1_benchmark.vcf.gz (public, complete,
    downloaded in full — 156MB), filtered to chr20 -> 85,951 real variant positions
    (ID column = "." everywhere, as expected for a truth set — no native
    dbSNP annotation, hence cross-referencing by POSITION rather than by ID).
  - dbSNP: refsnp-chr20.json.bz2 (~9.0GB compressed total), 400MB compressed chunk taken
    from the start of the file (HTTP range-fetch, HTTP 206), streamed decompression
    (~8GB decompressed, no intermediate disk write) -> 315,948 RefSNP
    records parsed (1 final line truncated by the chunk cut, ignored).

Cross-reference method: for each RefSNP record with a placement on
NC_000020.11 (chr20), SPDI position(s) (0-based) converted to VCF position (1-based),
then intersected with the 85,951 real GIAB positions. Known limitation:
position-only matching, not exact-allele (approximate for indels, reliable for SNVs).

=> On this chunk (which already covers 76% of real HG002 chr20 positions), nearly
   3/4 of the real variants in the HG002 reference genome sit on an rsID that has
   historically absorbed one or more merged former identifiers. This is
   the equivalent, at chr20 scale, of Paper A's "39/56" ratio (taxid) — same
   mechanism, different identifier system (rsID vs taxid), different domain
   (clinical germline genomics vs. viral metagenomics).

LIMITATIONS TO KEEP IN MIND (see also README.md):
  - This chunk only covers the ~316k oldest rsIDs (file sorted by increasing rsID,
    not by position) -> the 24% of GIAB positions not touched here would probably
    be covered by more recent rsIDs (higher build/number) in the rest of the
    9GB file, not downloaded in this session.
  - Composition bias: these old (pre-~2010) rsIDs are over-represented by common/
    well-studied variants -> the 73.79% rate might not generalize as-is to the
    remaining 24% of positions (likely rarer/more recently cataloged variants,
    presumably less often merged since younger in dbSNP's history).
  - Position-only matching, not yet verified allele-by-allele (REF/ALT).
