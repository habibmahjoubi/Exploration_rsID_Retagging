DEFINITIVE results: stale-identifier rate on full coverage (25.9M records)
=============================================================================================

This file supersedes/finalizes results/05, 06 and 07 -- the first "downstream
impact" panel test built on FULL coverage of dbSNP chr20
(candidate_panel_FULLCOV_allele_exact.csv, 48,746 candidates, exact-allele,
all merge histories combined), rather than the biased partial sample
(315,939 records) used until now.

METHOD
-------
Full panel rebuilt in a single pass (scripts/build_full_coverage_panel.py,
~1h10 of compute, curl|bzip2|python stream with no intermediate storage): for
EVERY chr20 RefSNP record (25,938,655 total), extraction of the real
SPDI alleles (SNV only) and exact matching against the 73,404
SNV alleles of the GIAB HG002 chr20 VCF. "2014-2017 merge" subset extracted:
**2,894 candidates** (versus 2,657 on the partial sample).

RESULT 1 -- overall merge rate, exact-allele, full coverage
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
chr20 dbSNP records parsed (ENTIRE file)          : 25,938,655
Exact-allele GIAB matches (any merge status)      : 73,370
  -> WITH a merge history                          : 48,746
  -> WITHOUT a merge history                        : 24,624

**FINAL MERGE RATE (exact-allele, full coverage): 66.44%**

This figure supersedes the previous 47.78% (which used POSITION-only
matching, hence noisier -- a position can carry several different
alleles, some merged and some not, which diluted the real rate). The
66.44% exact-allele figure is the most rigorous obtained to
date, and is closer to the initial estimate (73.79%, itself
biased by sampling) than the intermediate 47.78% figure. Full history
of the 3 estimates:
  73.79% (partial sample, position-only)             -- superseded
  47.78% (full coverage, position-only)               -- superseded
  66.44% (full coverage, EXACT-ALLELE)                 -- DEFINITIVE

RESULT 2 -- bundle stale-identifier rate, full unbiased panel (n=2,894)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                  dbSNP138 (2014)    dbSNP144 (June 2015)
Position+allele absent from bundle    19                  31
Current ID (up to date)            2,506 (86.6%)       2,758 (95.3%)
STALE ID                              369 (12.75%)        105 (3.63%)
Different ID (same allele)              0                   0

=> The rates increase relative to the partial sample (8.24%->12.75% and
   1.73%->3.63%) -- the biased sample (old rsIDs) was actually slightly
   UNDER-estimating the real risk. The temporal trend remains clear and in the
   same direction (~3.5x drop between the two bundles, versus ~4.8x before --
   same qualitative conclusion, slightly different magnitude).
=> The "different identifier" category stays at 0 for both bundles,
   confirming that exact-allele matching fully explains this
   previously mysterious category.

CONCLUSION
----------
The "downstream impact" panel is now built on FULL coverage
of dbSNP chr20, no longer a partial sample. This is the definitive result
to cite in the manuscript: **12.75% of real variants with a 2014-2017 merge
are reported with a stale identifier by the GATK dbSNP138 bundle**,
versus 3.63% for the more recent dbSNP144 bundle.

Still open: a 3rd version point (dbSNP151/152/current) blocked by the
proprietary Illumina Nirvana (.nsa) format; the real run of Article 1's
sarek pipeline in place of the GIAB truth VCF.
